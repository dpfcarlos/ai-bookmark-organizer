Bookmark Organizer

Clean up and organize your browser bookmarks. Everything runs in this page: your bookmarks are never uploaded.

1. Export

In your browser, open the bookmarks manager and export them to an .html file.

2. Organize here

Load the file, remove duplicates and dead entries, sort and rearrange.

3. Load the clean file

Download the new file, delete the old bookmarks in the browser and import the new one.

Drop your bookmarks .html file here, or click to choose

You can load several files at once (for example from two browsers); they are merged.

How do I export my bookmarks?

Tip: double-click a name to rename it. Click the arrow to open or close a folder.
